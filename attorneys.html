Advocate Sanjay Tiwari & Chambers Team

Two decades of dedicated legal practice defending client liberty, property rights, and commercial interests across Dhanbad Court and the High Court of Jharkhand.

Senior Trial Counsel • Civil & Criminal

Advocate Sanjay Tiwari

B.Sc., LL.B. • Practicing Advocate, Dhanbad Bar Association

Advocate Sanjay Tiwari is one of Dhanbad's most recognized and respected litigators in both civil property disputes and criminal trial defense. Enrolled with the Bar Council of Jharkhand with over 24 years of active courtroom practice, he has successfully argued more than 1,850 civil suits, sessions trials, and bail applications.

Renowned for his razor-sharp cross-examination skills, deep mastery of revenue khatiyan records, and strategic bail jurisprudence, he represents prominent business houses, property owners, and private individuals before Judicial Magistrates, Sessions Judges, and the High Court of Jharkhand at Ranchi.

Bar Admissions: Jharkhand State Bar Council, Dhanbad Bar Association (Life Member).
