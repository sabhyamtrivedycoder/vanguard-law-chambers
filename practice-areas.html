  • Sessions Trial Defense in BNS / IPC Offenses (Murder, Assault, Dacoity)

  • Police Custody Remand Hearings & Chargesheet Quashing

Stay Orders & Specific Performance

  • Suits for Specific Performance of Agreement to Sell
