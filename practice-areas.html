Specialized Courtroom Competence

Litigation & Appellate Practice Areas

Our chambers provide focused oral advocacy, strategic pleadings drafting, and comprehensive dispute representation across India's premier constitutional and economic benches.

PRACTICE 01 • CONSTITUTIONAL BENCH

Supreme Court Appellate Litigation & Writs

We specialize in Special Leave Petitions (SLPs under Article 136), Transfer Petitions, Statutory Appeals, and Original Writs under Article 32. Our senior advocates regularly argue matters involving fundamental rights, constitutional validity of statutory enactments, and presidential references.

  • Articles 32 and 226 High Court writ petitions
  • Civil & Criminal Special Leave Petitions (SLP)
  • Article 142 inherent justice invocations
  • Review and Curative Petitions
Brief Chambers on an SLP →
PRACTICE 02 • CORPORATE INSOLVENCY

Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (NCLT / NCLAT)

Defending corporate debtor boards, resolution applicants, and institutional creditor consortia in Section 7, 9, and 10 IBC proceedings. We navigate cross-border insolvency, resolution plan challenges, fraudulent preference actions, and director disqualification defense.

  • Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) advocacy
  • Resolution Plan approval & objections before NCLAT
  • Avoidance and fraudulent transaction defense (Sections 43, 66)
  • Liquidation asset distribution litigation
Consult on IBC Defense →
PRACTICE 03 • WHITE-COLLAR DEFENSE

Enforcement Directorate, CBI & PMLA Defense

White-collar enforcement actions demand immediate, proactive constitutional intervention. We represent C-suite executives, promoters, and corporate enterprises facing Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) attachments, CBI chargesheets, and SFIO summons.

  • Anticipatory and regular bail under strict PMLA Section 45
  • Challenging provisional attachment orders before Appellate Tribunal
  • Quashing of FIRs under Section 482 CrPC before High Courts
  • Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) adjudication
Confidential Defense Retainer →
PRACTICE 04 • DISPUTE RESOLUTION

International Commercial Arbitration

From emergency arbitrator relief and Section 9 interim measures to full evidentiary hearings and Section 34/37 award enforcement or challenges under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

  • Representation under SIAC, LCIA, ICC, and DIAC arbitration rules
  • Section 11 Chief Justice appointment petitions
  • Enforcement of foreign arbitral awards (New York Convention)
  • Bilateral Investment Treaty (BIT) claims
