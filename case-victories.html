Courtroom Victories & Relief Decrees

Demonstrated track record of successful trial outcomes, honorable criminal acquittals, anticipatory bails, and landmark civil land decrees argued by Advocate Sanjay Tiwari.

Title Suit No. 118/2018 • Decreed

Sahu & Ors. v. Mahato & Anr. (14-Acre Land Title Suit)

Forum: Court of Senior Civil Judge, Dhanbad
14 Acres
Full Title Decreed

Advocate Sanjay Tiwari represented the original raiyat heirs in a complex ancestral land dispute involving contested mutation entries and forged sale deeds. Through meticulous presentation of historical survey khatiyan records and persuasive examination of local revenue witnesses, the court held that defendants held no title, decreeing full proprietary possession and perpetual injunction under Order 39 CPC in favor of our clients.

Key Subject: Chota Nagpur Tenancy Act, Section 34 & 38 Specific Relief Act, Khatiyan Revenue Rebuttal.
Sessions Trial No. 242/2021 • Acquittal

State of Jharkhand v. Sunil Singh & Ors. (IPC 302/34 Defense)

Forum: Additional Sessions Judge - III, Dhanbad
Honorable
Full Acquittal

Defended three individuals falsely roped into a village factional murder chargesheet. During cross-examination of the primary eyewitness and autopsy surgeon, Advocate Sanjay Tiwari exposed critical contradictions between ocular evidence and medical injury timelines, proving the FIR was ante-timed and fabricated. The Sessions Court acquitted all accused persons with complete exoneration.

Key Subject: Section 302/120B IPC, Forensic Cross-Examination, Ocular vs Medical Contradiction.
ABA No. 3409/2022 • Relief Allowed

Rameshwar Yadav v. State of Jharkhand (Anticipatory Bail)

Forum: High Court of Jharkhand (Ranchi Bench)
Bail Allowed
Pre-Arrest Protection

Represented a prominent coal transportation operator implicated under Mines & Minerals Act and Section 414 IPC. Successfully established before the High Court of Jharkhand that all transport challans and weighbridge e-way bills were authentic, demonstrating lack of mens rea and malafide police action. Anticipatory bail was granted unconditionally.

Key Subject: Section 438 CrPC, Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, E-Way Bill Verification.
Complaint Case No. 845/2020 • Conviction & Recovery

Apex Industrial Corp v. Eastern Engineering Ltd. (Section 138 NI Act)

Forum: Court of Chief Judicial Magistrate (CJM), Dhanbad
₹85 Lakh
Recovery & Damages

Prosecuted a series of dishonoured commercial cheques against a recalcitrant contracting firm. Rebutted the statutory defense under Section 139 NI Act through ledger accounts and purchase confirmations. The court sentenced the managing director to civil imprisonment and awarded ₹85 Lakh in direct compensation.

Key Subject: Section 138 & 139 Negotiable Instruments Act, Legal Debt Presumption, Summary Execution.
