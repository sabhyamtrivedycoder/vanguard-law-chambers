Judicial Precedents

Landmark Judgments on Record

Documented appellate victories shaping commercial jurisprudence, constitutional protections, and white-collar defense in India.

Reported: (2024) 4 SCC 182

Consortium of Telecom Operators v. Union of India & Anr.

Forum: Supreme Court of India (Five-Judge Constitutional Bench)
₹4,200 Cr
Penalty Demand Quashed

Senior Counsel Vikramaditya Roy led arguments on behalf of the infrastructure consortium. The Constitutional Bench held that administrative penalty clauses cannot be applied retrospectively where licensees operated under unambiguous legitimate policy representations, reinforcing doctrine of promissory estoppel against sovereign fiscal authorities.

Key Subject: Article 14, Article 19(1)(g), Promissory Estoppel in Sovereign Concession Agreements.
Reported: 2023 DHC 6291

BioVanguard AG (Switzerland) v. Apex Pharma Formulations

Forum: High Court of Delhi (Intellectual Property Division)
48 Hours
Ad-Interim Injunction

Managing Partner Ananya Deshmukh moved an emergency application before the newly constituted IP Division. The court granted comprehensive Anton Piller and ad-interim injunction orders protecting proprietary compound patents from unauthorized commercial launch across 14 territorial states.

Key Subject: Section 48 & 108 Patents Act 1970, Quia Timet Action, Ex-Parte Restraining Orders.
NCLAT Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 412/2023

State Bank Consortium v. Resolution Applicant & Anr.

Forum: National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT, New Delhi)
₹6,800 Cr
Debt Plan Approved

Represented the Committee of Creditors against dissenting operational claimants attempting to stay implementation of an approved energy infrastructure resolution plan. NCLAT upheld commercial wisdom of CoC and dismissed Section 61 appeal with costs.

Key Subject: Section 30(2) and Section 31 Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016, Commercial Wisdom of CoC.
