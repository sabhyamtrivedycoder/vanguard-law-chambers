Professional Privilege

Attorney-Client Privilege Policy

The fiduciary trust between litigant and legal counsel is protected under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

1. Absolute Confidentiality Under Indian Law

All oral communications, case papers, FIR copies, title deeds, and briefing notes shared with Advocate Sanjay Tiwari and his chambers are protected by statutory attorney-client privilege under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 and the Bar Council of India Rules of Professional Standards.

2. Security of Title Deeds & Trial Evidence

Original revenue khatiyans, registered sale deeds, bank instruments, and police chargesheets entrusted to our chambers are maintained in secure physical file vaults at Chamber No. 28, Bar Association Building, Dhanbad Court.

3. Non-Disclosure in Preliminary Consultations

Even if a matter does not culminate in formal vakalatnama engagement, all factual details disclosed during the initial preliminary briefing remain confidential and will never be disclosed to opposite parties or third parties.

4. Chambers Inquiry
