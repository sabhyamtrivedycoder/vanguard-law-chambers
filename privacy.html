Professional Secrecy

Attorney-Client Privilege Policy

The sacred trust between counsel and client is protected under Sections 126 through 129 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872.

1. Absolute Confidentiality Under Indian Law

All communications, briefing memos, financial disclosures, and draft petitions transmitted to Vanguard & Juris are privileged and immune from disclosure under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872, and the Bar Council of India Rules on Professional Ethics.

2. Digital Security & Case File Vaults

Our chambers utilize air-gapped internal document management servers with AES-256 encryption. Access to sensitive litigation dockets is restricted strictly to designated senior advocates, briefed associates, and the registry clerk handling the matter.

3. Non-Disclosure in Preliminary Consultations

Even if a client does not ultimately formalize an engagement or retain our chambers, any preliminary factual disclosure made during initial conflict checks and assessment remains strictly privileged and will never be shared with opposing parties or third-party institutions.

4. Registry Contact

For inquiries regarding file custody or certified archival retrieval, write to: privilege@vanguardjuris.com.
